What can I bring?

Interactive guide for carry‑on, checked baggage, and customs rules.

Disclaimer: This is an informational prototype. Rules change and vary by airline and airport. Always verify with your airline, airport security (TSA/EU), and official customs websites. Not legal advice.

Select origin and destination, then type an item to see rules.
